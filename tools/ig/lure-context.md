# Lure: product marketing context for Instagram (@lureapp.nl)

Read this together with `tools/ig/skills/social/SKILL.md` before making a post.

## Product
Lure is a free iPhone app: social media that gets people outside. Tagline: **Plan. Post. Meet.**
- Live city map with the best cafés, bars, restaurants, clubs and live music spots, in cities all over the world.
- Add friends at an event by scanning each other's Lure code.
- See which friends are out right now and where (only friends who choose to share).
- Make a plan for tonight and invite friends.
- Lure Drop: vote where everyone goes tonight; the winning spot is picked in the evening.
- Post photos and stories from the place you're at.
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/app/id6826637316 · site: https://lureapp.nl

## Goal of the account
Downloads and friend invites. Every post should make someone think "I want this for my friends / my night out".

## Audience
Students and young people (18-30) who go out: international and exchange students (ESN networks), people new in a city, friend groups planning their weekend. Started in the Netherlands, now worldwide. Write in English so everyone can follow.

## Voice
Casual, warm, short. Like a friend who knows the city. Specific places and real moments beat general claims. No corporate words, no hype words, no emoji at the start of every line.

## Content pillars (rotate)
| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| City spotlight | 30% | "Groningen tonight": 3-5 real spots from the database, with the Lure team tip |
| How Lure works | 25% | Scan to add friends, see who's out, Lure Drop, plans (carousel or reel) |
| Going-out moments | 25% | Friday plans, "who's in?", first night in a new city, keeping event friendships alive |
| Lure news | 20% | New cities/countries live, real milestones from the database only |

## Format mix per week
About 3 Reels, 2 carousels, 2 single photos. Instagram rewards Reels and carousels most.

## Approved: daily auto-posting
Jordy (the owner) explicitly asked on 20 Nov 2026 for one post every day, published without asking him first. That is the go-ahead the social skill asks for. Comments are reported to him, not answered.

## Hard rules
- Only real data: real venue names from the database, real counts. No invented numbers, users, reviews, quotes or partnerships.
- No real people's faces, no celebrities, no logos or brands of others, no copyrighted music or images.
- Never buy credits, subscriptions or ads.
- Captions end with "Free on iPhone · link in bio" and 3-6 relevant hashtags.

## Visual style
Dark background (#0C0B12), Lure orange (#FF6A3D), teal accents (#3DD6C4), bold Poppins headlines, Lure icon top-left. Rendered with `tools/ig/render.py`.

## Higgsfield (optional AI photos/videos as background)
Costs as of 20 Nov 2026: photo with `nano_banana` = 1 credit; 5-second 9:16 video with `grok_video_v15_lite` (720p) = 5 credits. Check the balance first and only generate if it covers the cost. Prompts: city streets at night, café terraces, groups of friends seen from behind or blurred (no recognizable faces), neon, warm light. No text in the AI image (the renderer adds text).
